Meeting Highlights Full Meeting Report ›

✅ What's Working / What Landed
  • 🎯SMART Goal Framework ▶ 4:05 — Both advisors independently cited goal-setting as the core NSLS value they would never want to lose. Dr. Paladino called it the foundational reason she was drawn to the organization.
  • 🌟Inspirational Video Content ▶ 10:11 — LaDonnia praised the motivational and inspirational quality of NSLS videos, noting they 'get the teams fired up.' The legitimacy factor of figures like Barack Obama was also affirmed.
  • 🤝National Team Goal Review Support ▶ 7:37 — Dr. Paladino explicitly valued the national team's review of student-submitted goals and work, stating it was something she could not manage alone and did not want to lose.
  • 🎨Society Rebrand & Visual Design ▶ 56:39 — LaDonnia spontaneously praised the Society rebrand, calling it 'fresh and modern' and noting the colors and typography felt more visually appealing to their student demographic.
  • 💡Clarity Track / Career Direction Tool ▶ 37:57 — LaDonnia responded enthusiastically to the Clarity track demo, saying 'I really think this will be so helpful for students just to get them started' and drew a direct comparison to the Working Genius assessment she uses.
❌ What Isn't Working
  • 📉SNT Attendance & Engagement Drop-Off ▶ 6:51 — Advisors noted disappointing turnout at SNTs and confirmed they are already considering a format restructure. The major drop-off point between Leadership Training Day and SNTs was acknowledged as a known platform-wide problem.
  • ❓Missing 'Why' for Early-Stage Students ▶ 17:45 — LaDonnia described the disconnect for first- and second-year students with no work experience who are undeclared and don't see themselves as leaders — they have no motivating reason to engage with NSLS content.
  • 🎞️Video Content Not Modular or Adaptive ▶ 8:18 — Both advisors agreed the video content, while inspirational, has not been updated to work flexibly for different contexts. Long-form videos with limited interactivity reduce in-event engagement.
  • 🏫Workforce Readiness Siloed by School ▶ 15:05 — LaDonnia identified that workforce readiness initiatives like VMOC are isolated to individual schools like Lee Business School, leaving students in other disciplines without access to equivalent career preparation tools.
  • 💸Long-Term Cost & Institutional Sustainability Concern ▶ 55:36 — Dr. Paladino held back from a 10/10 score specifically because of uncertainty about future pricing and whether the university would support and fund Society as it scales.
⚠️ Key Signal▶ 42:07 — LaDonnia Petersen: High-Value Product Thinker and Immediate Champion: LaDonnia gave Society a 10/10 excitement score, confirmed pilot interest on the spot, and demonstrated product-level thinking throughout — asking whether the career readiness score adapts by track, whether the AI coach tailors to UNLV's course catalog, and how induction credit would work under the new model. She also surfaced a direct competitive comparison with VMOC, flagging specific UX limitations in that tool that Society could address. Her engagement pattern — proactive, detailed, solutions-oriented — signals strong potential as a Product Council contributor and internal advocate for Society at UNLV.
