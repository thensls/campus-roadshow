School & Chapter Snapshot

About St. Philip's College
  • 🏫St. Philip's College is a public two-year institution in San Antonio, TX, holding both HBCU and HSI designations and operating as part of the Alamo Colleges District, where each college maintains its own independent NSLS chapter. ▶ 25:16
  • 🏥The NSLS chapter operates under the Department of Student Success but is advised by faculty on the academic side, creating a structural hybrid unique within the district. ▶ 9:29

✅ What's Working / What Landed
  • 🌱Student Belonging and Momentum ▶ 16:12 — Amanda Roberts emphasized that what must never be lost is the spirit of students feeling they belong and that NSLS is a lifelong stepping stone — not just a one-time accomplishment. She connected this directly to the idea of maintaining momentum in the product.
  • 🤖AI Coach and Career Clarity Track ▶ 28:12 — Amanda Roberts gave a 10/10 excitement score after seeing the Clarity Track and AI coach demo, signaling strong resonance with the career direction and personalization features.
  • 📱SNT Matching Automation ▶ 39:29 — The automated SNT matching feature directly addressed a known drop-off problem; while Miranda from NVC was the most vocal, the feature was discussed in the context of all Alamo Colleges chapters including SPC, and the 50% fall-off framing was acknowledged as a shared challenge.
❌ What Isn't Working
  • 📋Co-curricular Transcript Data Gap ▶ 46:25 — Morgan from Palo Alto raised that student participation in NSLS events needs to be captured on the Alamo Experience platform (Engagure) for co-curricular transcripts; this is a shared district-level infrastructure concern that affects SPC as part of Alamo Colleges.
  • 👤Advisor Bandwidth and Continuity Risk ▶ 9:20 — The SPC chapter recently lost its co-founding director Dr. Mack to retirement, leaving Ryan Garza as sole lead advisor. This transition creates a continuity risk and increased load on a single advisor without replacement support yet in place.
⚠️ Key Signal▶ 16:05 — Amanda Roberts as a High-Enthusiasm Early Advocate: Amanda Roberts gave a 10/10 excitement score unprompted and was the most expressive SPC voice during the product demo, framing Society's value in terms of student belonging and lifelong growth — language that maps directly to the platform's positioning. Her background in both science and program leadership, combined with her visible enthusiasm, makes her a strong candidate to drive internal buy-in at the Southwest Campus and potentially influence the broader SPC pilot decision. Ryan Garza's institutional depth as chapter co-founder adds credibility and stability, but Amanda represents the more immediate advocacy signal.
