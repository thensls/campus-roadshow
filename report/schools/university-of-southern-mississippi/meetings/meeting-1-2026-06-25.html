Section 3 — Discovery Findings

What They Want to Preserve

  • The strong, established NSLS chapter relationship at Southern Miss — noted by Gregg as one of his best accounts with demonstrated results over four years
  • The momentum and investment already made in Eagle Hub (Campus Groups) as the school's student engagement infrastructure
  • The quantitative approach to understanding the student experience — Dr. King wants to leverage data he now has access to through Eagle Hub
  • The prior institutional vision around co-curricular learning outcomes and NACE alignment, even if currently deprioritized — Dr. King expressed that this work matters and he was a key part of it▶ 6:51

What Isn't Working

  • Student attrition driven primarily by financial aid gaps and personal obligations (e.g., serving as primary caretakers for family members) — described as the core retention challenge
  • The prior focus on co-curricular learning outcomes and NACE competency alignment has been "pushed to the background" under the current leadership transition
  • Career readiness work outside the classroom is underdeveloped — Dr. King acknowledged "a great deal of headroom" and described current efforts as pockets of interest rather than a broad institutional push
  • Lack of longitudinal data on students — previously unable to quantify the co-curricular student experience in a meaningful way (Eagle Hub is meant to address this going forward)

School Priorities

  • Enrollment growth — explicitly named as the top institutional priority
  • Student retention — closely linked to enrollment; financial aid gaps are the primary lever to address
  • Digital student engagement — Eagle Hub implementation is a current, active institutional initiative with high visibility
  • Career readiness — identified as important and aspirational, with the "Ready for Life" marketing campaign, but acknowledged as an area with significant room to grow outside the classroom
  • NACE competency alignment — career services is interested; was previously part of a broader co-curricular learning outcomes initiative Dr. King was leading

Section 4 — Key Themes & Signals

1. Data Hunger Is a Primary Driver

Dr. King explicitly described himself as having a quantitative background but lacking the data to leverage it — until now. Eagle Hub is the current vehicle for that. Society's student engagement dashboard and career readiness score landed as directly relevant to this need. The framing of "Michael King in your pocket" as an advisor intelligence tool resonated clearly. He will respond well to any feature that surfaces actionable, student-level data.

2. Financial Barriers Are the Core Retention Problem

Approximately half of Southern Miss students are Pell-eligible or receive financial assistance. When students leave, it is most often tied to finances or family caregiving responsibilities. This is the institution's most pressing student success challenge. Society's career readiness and clarity features are indirectly relevant here — helping students see a clearer path to employment may reinforce persistence — but no direct connection was made in the meeting.

3. Career Readiness Has Institutional Buy-In but Lacks Infrastructure

Dr. King confirmed that career readiness is valued — the school markets it with the "Ready for Life" tagline — but acknowledged that execution outside the classroom is thin. Career services wants NACE competency alignment, and Dr. King was previously driving a co-curricular learning outcomes initiative that would have intersected with this. Society's Career Clarity track was positioned as a low-lift solution for exactly this gap, and Dr. King was receptive.

4. Healthy Skepticism Toward Product Promises

When told Society could support NACE alignment with "very little heavy lifting," Dr. King's immediate response was: "I wouldn't believe you on that — number one."▶ 9:49 His 8–9 enthusiasm score included a candid acknowledgment that the remaining points were skepticism about new products generally. This is not resistance — it is a signal that proof of delivery will matter more than the pitch. Pilot access was the right move to address this directly.

5. Integration with Eagle Hub Is a Natural Ask

Dr. King is actively building out Eagle Hub (Campus Groups) as the school's student engagement platform, including event calendaring, student org pages, badging, and gamification. When Chris raised the idea of an integration between Society and their student engagement platform, Dr. King's response was immediate: "I would love that."▶ 21:21 This is a meaningful signal — not polite agreement, but an expression of genuine operational interest.

  • The advisor intelligence dashboard — specifically the idea of seeing a career readiness score before a student meeting so the advisor knows where to focus ("hey, I reviewed your report, and here are some things we ought to talk about")
  • The concise AI coach responses — Dr. King noticed and commented positively that the coach didn't "go on and on" like a typical LLM▶ 17:26
  • The goal-setting and SMART goal integration — connecting back to Foundations of Leadership content felt coherent and purposeful
  • Longitudinal data capability — Dr. King has been limited by semester-only views; multi-cohort data was noted as addressing a known frustration▶ 20:40
  • The pilot access offer — Dr. King immediately named internal colleagues and departments he would want to bring in

What Raised Questions or Friction

  • Skepticism about the "low-lift" promise for NACE alignment — Dr. King pushed back directly on this claim and will need to validate it through use
  • CliftonStrengths integration — Dr. King asked specifically whether "strengths" referred to CliftonStrengths; the answer was not yet but potentially in the future.▶ 15:14 This is a tool already familiar to him and likely in use at Southern Miss
  • General new-product skepticism — explicitly stated; not a blocker, but requires proof-of-concept follow-through this summer

Surprises

  • He immediately began mentally populating a list of internal champions before the meeting ended▶ 22:24, suggesting strong internal network awareness and genuine intent to move forward
