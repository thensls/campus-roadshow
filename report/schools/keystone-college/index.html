✅ What's Working / What Landed
  • 📱NSLS App & Checklists ▶ 7:54 — Carl Hicks cited the existing NSLS app as something he values; Lucas Taylor specifically highlighted the induction checklists as clear and useful for students tracking their progress.
  • 🤝CSR Relationship with Dejeahn ▶ 6:50 — Lucas Taylor credited their CSR Dejeahn Brown for resolving early onboarding friction, describing the current support experience as 'seamless.'
  • 🎯AI Career Coach Demo ▶ 30:51 — Carl Hicks responded positively to the AI coach, calling it 'neat' and 'a very useful tool,' particularly in the context of helping students find career direction.
❌ What Isn't Working
  • 📧Student Communication Channels ▶ 11:39 — Carl Hicks described a persistent challenge reaching students — email goes unread and a texting service prompted opt-outs, leaving advisors uncertain about what engagement channel actually works.
  • 🧭Advisor Familiarity with NSLS System ▶ 37:19 — Hicks noted that during their first year, members had questions the advisors couldn't answer because they weren't yet familiar enough with the NSLS system to guide students properly.
  • 💼Career Services Instability ▶ 10:36 — Carl Hicks acknowledged repeated turnover in the career services office, creating inconsistency in career readiness programming for students over recent years.
⚠️ Key Signal▶ 39:17 — Retention-Driven Buy-In Tied to Career Readiness Data: Carl Hicks explicitly connected Society's career readiness score and profile tools to Keystone's top institutional priority — student retention — framing it as a way to help students see a path beyond Keystone and feel proud of something unique to their experience there. He also noted that the platform could help advisors stop fielding procedural questions and instead engage students more meaningfully. Given Keystone's U.S. News upward mobility ranking and reliance on that narrative for enrollment, the career readiness score as a reportable metric represents a high-value, strategically aligned data point for the institution.
