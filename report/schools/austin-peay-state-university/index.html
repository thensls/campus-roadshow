✅ What's Working / What Landed
  • 🏆Induction Step Challenge Model ▶ 6:01 — Janesa noted students appreciate having concrete steps to complete, distinguishing NSLS from other honor societies on campus where membership is passive. The structured challenge keeps members motivated and engaged.
  • 🙋In-Person Programming This Spring ▶ 7:00 — After virtual Zoom broadcasts underperformed in Fall, the chapter shifted to fully in-person events this Spring and saw strong attendance across all programming, with students visibly enjoying the experience.
  • 🎭Supplemental Events Beyond Induction ▶ 7:04 — Events like community service, a de-stress event, campus cleanup, homecoming participation, and an open general board meeting keep inducted members engaged and visible on campus after completing required steps.
  • 🔢Advisor-Driven Data Tracking ▶ 15:29 — Janesa self-tracks chapter numbers semester-over-semester using a competitive mindset from CliftonStrengths, benchmarking completion rates and attendance even without formal institutional SLO requirements.
❌ What Isn't Working
  • 👥SNT Group Confusion for Commuter and Deployed Students ▶ 4:43 — The wording and structure of SNT groups in the portal causes confusion, especially for commuter students and active-duty members who are deployed and cannot schedule Zoom calls with APSU peers. Janesa manually works around this by advising deployed students to form groups with people around them.
  • ⏱️Manual Attendance Credit Assignment ▶ 41:42 — Janesa spends approximately 30 minutes per event manually entering student names across two monitors to assign orientation and leadership training day credit in the NSLS portal, creating a bottleneck — especially during in-person SNT matching at the end of Leadership Training Day.
  • 📧Student Email and Professional Readiness Gap ▶ 14:14 — Advisors are actively teaching students basic professional skills like reading and replying to emails. Janesa attributed this to COVID disrupting middle and high school norms, creating a backlog of career readiness skills that must be addressed before students can fully engage with NSLS steps.
  • 📦Two-App Experience for Society Transition ▶ 49:05 — Janesa raised a direct concern about Society being a second downloadable app separate from the existing NSLS app, noting that students currently prefer and frequently use the existing app. The interim two-app model may create friction for adoption.
⚠️ Key Signal▶ 40:12 — Attendance Integration Bottleneck Blocks Real-Time SNT Matching: Janesa identified a concrete workflow conflict: APSU uses Ready Education with mobile ID scanners for university event attendance, and she must spend roughly 30 minutes manually cross-referencing two monitors to assign NSLS credit after each event. When Chris proposed using Society's SNT matching tool at the end of Leadership Training Day, Janesa immediately flagged that she would be too occupied applying attendance credit to actually facilitate the matching — making real-time in-person SNT formation unworkable under the current flow. This is not unique to APSU; Chris acknowledged it as a broadly applicable problem and flagged it as a priority feedback item for the product team. A follow-up meeting was confirmed to explore what an integrated or streamlined attendance-to-SNT workflow would need to look like.
