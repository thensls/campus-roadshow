Meeting Highlights Full Meeting Report ›

✅ What's Working / What Landed
  • ⚙️Centralized Platform Operations ▶ 7:58 — Dan values having the ability to manage member experience through a single platform that both advisors and students interact with, calling it helpful for operational consistency.
  • 🌐Flexible Programming Model ▶ 6:24 — Dan highlighted that NSLS's flexible programming — including both in-person and online options — reaches students who would not otherwise engage in campus involvement, which he identified as a core strength to preserve.
❌ What Isn't Working
  • 📉Cross-Semester Data Loss ▶ 9:00 — Induction rate data resets before the semester even ends, making it impossible to compare data across semesters or years without maintaining separate personal spreadsheets. Dan explicitly noted he submitted this concern in a prior NSLS survey.
  • 😟Low Completion and Engagement Rates ▶ 52:09 — Dan expressed long-standing concern that more than half of paying members do not complete the program, and that he has not felt urgency at the national level to address this drop-off. He framed this as both a student value and institutional integrity issue.
  • ❓Student Onboarding Clarity ▶ 52:54 — Dan raised concern about how the new layers of Society content will be introduced to students given that Foundations of Leadership is already a lot to communicate. He questioned how everything will be delivered in a clear and digestible way.
⚠️ Key Signal▶ 9:00 — Accountability Gap: Paying Members Not Completing: Dan Balski articulated a direct concern that has persisted over years: students pay for NSLS membership, yet more than half do not complete the program, and he has not felt national-level urgency to solve it. He currently compensates with his own spreadsheet tracking because the platform does not support cross-semester data comparison — induction data even resets before the semester ends. His 9/10 excitement for Society (10/10 on content) was tempered only by questions about whether the new platform's onboarding will be clear enough for students to navigate the additional layers alongside Foundations of Leadership.
