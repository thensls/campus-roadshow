Meeting Highlights Full Meeting Report ›

✅ What's Working / What Landed
  • 🏆 ▶ 40:42 — Connie noted that students join specifically for the leadership identity and honors credentials — one student in a Leadership Training Day said they joined 'for the cords.' The NSLS brand and recognition precedes the program and motivates students to join.
  • 🎯 ▶ 31:45 — Connie immediately saw the Clarity Track as a strong fit for older non-traditional students and trades-focused students who are coming to SPCC to get a skill and go to work, not necessarily to transfer to a four-year institution.
  • 🧠 ▶ 24:57 — Connie noted that personality surveys like those in the Insights Track are 'very expensive' outside of NSLS and appreciated that they would be built into the platform, calling them great tools for students.
  • 🤖 ▶ 54:27 — Connie expressed relief at the idea of the AI coach sending reminders and accountability nudges to students who haven't completed steps, reducing the burden of her personally sending repeated follow-up emails.
❌ What Isn't Working
  • 📧 ▶ 9:30 — Connie explained that the orientation video mixes messaging for in-person and online chapters, causing confusion. Students who don't attend in person hear content directed at online-only members and assume they can complete everything on their own timeline, leading to incomplete steps.
  • 📊 ▶ 48:23 — Connie said the old dashboard allowed her to track and report on community service projects, fundraising, and other chapter activities. That data is now gone, leaving her only able to see enrollment and completion counts — making it harder to demonstrate chapter success to college leadership.
  • ⚖️ ▶ 41:11 — Connie raised a concern that students — especially those motivated by honors prestige — might view the Clarity Track as a lesser or inferior path compared to Foundations of Leadership. She emphasized that positioning would be critical so it doesn't feel like a downgrade.
  • 📋 ▶ 7:51 — Connie recalled that a previous requirement to submit a detailed strategic plan each semester — including forecasted speaker broadcast dates — was long, cumbersome, and difficult to complete accurately. She was glad it had been removed.
⚠️ Key Signal▶ 17:09 — Trades & Non-Traditional Students Are an Untapped Clarity Track Audience: Connie explicitly flagged that SPCC's student body includes a large population of trades students and older non-traditional learners who come to get a skill and go directly to work — not to transfer or pursue leadership titles. She sees the Clarity Track as a compelling entry point for these students who may not respond to the Foundations of Leadership framing. However, she cautioned that the track must be positioned carefully so it doesn't feel inferior to the FOL path, particularly given that SPCC's early college and dual-enrollment students are highly motivated by honors recognition and credential prestige. This tension between honors-driven students and career-focused trades students represents a critical segmentation challenge for how Society is marketed and rolled out at community colleges.
