✅ What's Working / What Landed
  • 🌐 ▶ 11:03 — Hunter affirmed that supporting students across in-person, online, and hybrid formats is critical, and expressed that maintaining strong in-person networking options is a key retention driver members explicitly value above other campus programs.
  • 🔗 ▶ 21:35 — Hunter noted that multiple members have told him NSLS was their best campus experience specifically because of the networking and people in the room, positioning community as the irreplaceable differentiator versus free campus alternatives.
  • 🎓 ▶ 17:26 — Hunter proactively raised the opportunity for alumni-facing products, continuing education credits, and mentorship pathways, describing it as money being left on the table and signaling strong alignment with Society's long-term roadmap.
❌ What Isn't Working
  • 📉 ▶ 10:45 — Hunter reported a marked dip in chapter engagement directly tied to offering online options, noting students choose virtual for convenience but then do not complete, creating confusion about steps and process across modalities.
  • 🏫 ▶ 20:56 — UT Knoxville is investing tens of millions in a near-identical leadership and career readiness program that claimed 17,000 student engagements last semester, forcing Hunter to actively think through how NSLS differentiates and justifies its value proposition.
  • 🤐 ▶ 58:37 — Hunter flagged that well-resourced departments like career services have big egos and prefer to operate independently, making cross-departmental buy-in for NSLS collaboration a significant practical challenge.
⚠️ Key Signal▶ 43:51 — Competitive Pressure Meets Collaboration Opportunity — Hunter as a Strategic Bridge: Hunter Hammock represents a uniquely high-stakes deployment context: UT Knoxville is simultaneously one of NSLS's most engaged chapter campuses and one where a well-funded institutional competitor is actively encroaching on NSLS's core value proposition. Hunter's questions about competitive differentiation and his candid acknowledgment of territorial campus departments signal that Society's data dashboard and cross-departmental collaboration narrative could be the most compelling unlock for this school. Hunter also identified Susanna Marshman, Assistant Vice Chancellor for Student Life and Professional Development, as a key campus contact — and Chris Higbee directly asked Hunter to facilitate that introduction, making this a live, time-sensitive action item.
