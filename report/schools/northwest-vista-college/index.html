About Northwest Vista College

  • 🏛️NVC is one of five colleges in the Alamo Colleges District; each college operates as its own NSLS chapter, and the district uses a co-curricular tracking platform called Alamo Experience, formerly Campus Labs, now Engkura Engage. ▶ 48:56

Meeting Highlights Full Meeting Report ›

✅ What's Working / What Landed
  • 🤝SNT Peer Accountability ▶ 13:24 — Miranda specifically called out SNTs as something that must never be lost, citing peer-to-peer engagement, vulnerability, and mutual support as core to their value.
  • 🎯Automated SNT Matching ▶ 41:16 — Miranda responded 'I'm amazed' and confirmed the 50% drop-off figure matched their own experience, calling the all-in-one chat, meeting, and matching system a direct solution to their biggest retention gap.
  • 💼Career Readiness & Cross-Department Collaboration ▶ 37:21 — Bets immediately saw Society's career features as a bridge to NVC's career experience team and career navigators, proactively suggesting those staff would need to 'dip their toe in' and be looped in.
  • ✨Modernized, Interactive Format ▶ 50:39 — Miranda said the platform makes her feel students are 'more inclined to finish it since it's more interactive and modernized,' and added it gives her a sense of hope for completion rates.
❌ What Isn't Working
  • 🚧GPA & Access Restrictions ▶ 17:45 — Bets flagged that campus culture under new leadership is questioning why NSLS has GPA eligibility requirements, with leadership asking why the program can't be open to all students.
  • 🔒Induction Gate on Society Access ▶ 52:22 — Miranda asked whether students must be inducted before unlocking Society, expressing implicit concern that the induction requirement could limit engagement given the chapter's current access and enrollment challenges.
  • 📋Leadership Transition & Institutional Uncertainty ▶ 17:38 — Both advisors noted significant turnover at the president, dean, and director levels, creating uncertainty about the chapter's future structure and the advisors' ability to commit to a pilot program.
  • 🗂️Co-Curricular Data Silos ▶ 46:25 — Bets clarified that their co-curricular tracking platform (Alamo Experience / Engkura Engage) is separate from NSLS, and there is currently no integration to pass student engagement data between systems.
⚠️ Key Signal▶ 37:21 — Career Team Integration Opportunity Identified Organically: Without prompting, Bets Murray immediately connected Society's career readiness features to NVC's career experience team and career navigators, suggesting those staff should 'dip their toe in' and see how they could support students through the platform. This was one of the clearest cross-departmental expansion signals in the session and mirrors the product team's own stated direction. If NVC moves forward as a pilot, engaging their career services staff early could accelerate institutional buy-in and help bridge the chapter's current vulnerability around leadership transition.
