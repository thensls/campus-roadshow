Meeting Highlights Full Meeting Report ›

✅ What's Working / What Landed
  • 🎯Career Clarity Track ▶ 17:57 — Nyeem responded with a '10 out of 10' rating and said the Clarity track was 'spot on' and 'head on' with his priorities. The concept of AI-guided dream job identification mapped directly onto his 'off-campus ready' framework.
  • 📈Career Readiness Score ▶ 53:43 — The career readiness score dashboard resonated strongly. Nyeem confirmed that measurable, shareable data is the key missing piece in his current work and called it 'a big box check.'
  • 🤖AI Coach Reducing Admin Burden ▶ 45:58 — Nyeem explicitly connected the AI coach to freeing him from resume reviews and repetitive tasks so he can focus on higher-impact human interactions. He said it would help him 'fine-tune' his own approach as well.
  • 👥SNT Matching Automation ▶ 41:21 — Nyeem confirmed SNT drop-off is a real problem and called the automated matching feature 'a very much intentional way of being that middle meat' students are missing. He saw it as directly solving the attention and workload barriers his students face.
  • 🏛️Faculty Buy-In Pathway ▶ 55:02 — Nyeem lit up when discussing how the career readiness data could be presented to faculty to earn their endorsement, noting students follow faculty who have 'skin in the game.' He was already naming faculty he would approach.
❌ What Isn't Working
  • 📧Invitation Perceived as Spam ▶ 9:32 — Nyeem identified the top engagement barrier as students dismissing NSLS invitations as scam emails. He said combating that perception is his current number-one priority for building student buy-in.
  • 🎬Dated and Long FOL Videos ▶ 20:46 — Chris confirmed and Nyeem implicitly acknowledged that existing Foundations of Leadership videos feel dated and are too long for students to stay engaged with, making facilitation difficult.
  • 🧭No Clear Student Action Plan ▶ 48:19 — Nyeem described students arriving with an idea of what to do but no real plan, leading to wasted time trying things that don't connect to a goal. He said students who don't see the 'middle meat' of a path simply fall off.
  • 🔗Fragmented Data Collection ▶ 13:08 — Career outcomes data is currently scattered across Handshake, FDS, and manual surveys with no unified view, making it hard to tell a coherent story of student success to faculty or administration.
⚠️ Key Signal▶ 55:02 — Doctoral Advisor Reinventing Career Services Commits on First Demo: Nyeem McDaniels, a doctoral student actively rebuilding Benedict's career services infrastructure ahead of a new department head, rated Society a 10/10 and confirmed pilot partnership within the same meeting. He immediately began identifying faculty he would recruit and framed Society as the data infrastructure for his 'off-campus ready' student outcome model. His framing — 'the career you want, not the career you fell into' and 'be that middle meat they're missing' — signals a high-articulation champion who will actively translate the platform's value to faculty and students alike.
