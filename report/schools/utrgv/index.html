Meeting Highlights Full Meeting Report ›

✅ What's Working / What Landed
  • 💻Current Platform Usability ▶ 7:14 — Both Brandon and Ashley independently praised the existing NSLS platform as user-friendly, clean, and step-by-step enough that students can navigate it without advisor guidance.
  • 📅SNT Scheduling Feature ▶ 39:33 — When the Society SNT scheduling and availability-matching feature was shown, Brandon immediately reacted positively and Ashley envisioned using aggregate availability data to pre-book campus rooms for student groups.
  • 🎨Visual Design of Society ▶ 45:53 — Brandon called the platform 'very pretty,' 'inviting,' and noted it 'catches their eye' — a meaningful signal given that visual appeal is one of the ways he judges whether students will engage.
  • 🎯Career Clarity Track Resonance ▶ 50:53 — Brandon said that as a recent student he would have loved a tool like Society, specifically connecting its value to career direction and helping students understand their values and readiness.
❌ What Isn't Working
  • 🚨NSLS Perceived as a Scam ▶ 12:07 — Brandon described students openly questioning whether NSLS is a scam even during orientation — stemming from a pattern of receiving unsolicited emails from multiple online leadership programs and a belief that anything requiring payment is fraudulent.
  • 🔄Hybrid Mode Friction ▶ 20:01 — Ashley flagged that switching between in-person and online participation requires students or staff to manually enable certain features or contact the team, creating friction that discourages completion.
  • 📧Campus Email as Primary Channel ▶ 48:32 — Students are missing or ignoring NSLS communications sent to campus email addresses; the team noted students prefer personal email or text, and the current system lacks that flexibility.
  • ⏰Enrollment Timing vs. Financial Aid ▶ 15:49 — Ashley observed anecdotally that enrollment spikes when recruitment deadlines align with financial aid disbursement, suggesting the current enrollment calendar may not be optimized around when students have funds available.
⚠️ Key Signal▶ 18:26 — Career Readiness as the Missing Hook for a Commuter-Heavy, Legitimacy-Skeptical Campus: UTRGV students are largely commuters who question whether NSLS is a scam and disengage before completing orientation — not because they lack interest, but because they don't see a clear personal payoff. Brandon explicitly said students want to know 'what's the benefit' and that a tangible outcome like career readiness or resume value is what would keep them engaged. Ashley immediately began mapping Society's career and mentoring features onto UTRGV's Freshman Leadership Academy, signaling that Society could serve as a structured post-freshman-year development pathway — a gap the school is actively trying to fill as it redevelops its leadership programming.
