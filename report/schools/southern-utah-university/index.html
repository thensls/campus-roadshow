Meeting Highlights Full Meeting Report ›

✅ What's Working / What Landed
  • 🎯Intentional Leadership Development ▶ 5:00 — Brandon specifically praised NSLS for its intentionality in moving students forward through structured leadership and career readiness programming, calling it a differentiator from other honor societies.
  • 🌐Cross-University SNT Networking ▶ 33:21 — Brandon lit up when he saw the SNT matching feature, noting it broadens student networks beyond their own university, which he called beautiful and directly tied to effective networking outcomes.
  • 📊Career Readiness Score Dashboard ▶ 43:31 — Brandon validated the career readiness score dashboard concept as exactly the data SUU is missing — showing measurable progress toward career learning outcomes over time.
❌ What Isn't Working
  • 💸Upsell Perception Damaging Legitimacy ▶ 6:29 — Brandon raised concern that NSLS's upsell offers — particularly credits that SUU does not honor — make students feel slighted and damage trust in the organization's legitimacy, and that these relationships are hard to repair.
  • 😕Lack of Clear Value Communication at Signup ▶ 7:06 — Brandon noted students sometimes don't fully understand what they signed up for, which compounds the legitimacy problem and contributes to disengagement early in the member journey.
⚠️ Key Signal▶ 11:50 — T-Bird Ready Alignment Opens Integration Opportunity: Brandon's T-Bird Ready program — built on eight competencies, evidence submission, SAR articulation, and a badging system — maps directly onto Society's career readiness score and achievement engine concepts. Chris explicitly flagged that Society could support T-Bird Ready functionality, and Brandon confirmed the career readiness score dashboard is precisely the data gap SUU is trying to close. This alignment positions SUU as a high-value pilot partner with a clear co-development opportunity around skill articulation and measurable career learning outcomes.
