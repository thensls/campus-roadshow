Meeting Highlights Full Meeting Report ›

✅ What's Working / What Landed
  • 🎯 ▶ 14:00 — Megan immediately connected the Clarity track to her existing frustration about students underutilizing career services and not thinking about careers from day one. She said, 'I could not have more perfectly teed up what I'm about to show you' was validated by Chris — her unprompted framing matched Society's core pitch almost exactly.
  • 🤖 ▶ 53:30 — Megan responded positively to the idea of the AI coach handling menial career prep tasks and freeing up the understaffed career services team, noting their chronic turnover and low student engagement.
  • 🔓 ▶ 49:08 — Megan engaged positively with the concept of locking the Clarity track until Foundations of Leadership is complete, framing it as a motivator for students to follow through on induction.
  • 📊 ▶ 9:14 — Megan, a former admissions director of 10 years, lit up at the promise of improved advisor analytics — cross-cohort reporting and the career readiness score concept directly addressed her stated need to understand where members drop off and how to intervene.
  • 🔌 ▶ 4:00 — Megan named the plug-and-play nature of NSLS programming as a key reason chapters like hers survive under time-constrained advisors — she specifically praised facilitation guides written so students could run events themselves.
❌ What Isn't Working
  • 📧 ▶ 8:33 — Megan flagged that the advisor dashboard only displays one semester at a time and resets mid-cycle, making it hard to track member conversion across cohorts. She noted this had been partially raised before and some fixes were made in winter.
  • 🧍 ▶ 44:06 — Megan described a pattern where students sign up, pay, then never complete — comparing NSLS to honor societies like Phi Theta Kappa where students assume paying the fee is the end of the commitment.
  • 📍 ▶ 41:48 — Students who cannot attend in person get stuck trying to find SNT partners with matching availability. Megan noted this is a significant drop-off point for her chapter.
  • 🎬 ▶ 47:05 — Megan shared that students question why they are sitting in a room watching videos they could access online, and that when tech failed she delivered orientation herself from memory — reflecting frustration with the scripted, video-dependent format.
  • 📉 ▶ 53:30 — Johnston CC's career services team cycles through turnover every one to two years, leaving the department solo for months at a time. Combined with students delaying engagement until just before graduation, the department struggles to make meaningful impact.
⚠️ Key Signal▶ 14:00 — Advisor Independently Articulated Society's Core Value Prop Before Seeing the Product: Before the demo began, Megan unprompted described her primary frustration as students not engaging career services until graduation and her desire to make NSLS 'more intentional as a career development program.' This so precisely matched Society's Clarity track pitch that Chris asked if Dejeahn had shared his notes. Megan confirmed Dejeahn had only said she needed to be 'in the room for this conversation.' This is a strong signal that Society's career clarity positioning resonates authentically with community college Dean-level pain — it was not sold to her, she arrived at it herself. Megan also surfaced the 8-week session transition as an additional reason asynchronous, self-paced career tools would be strategically valuable at her institution going into fall.
