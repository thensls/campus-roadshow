Meeting Highlights Full Meeting Report ›

✅ What's Working / What Landed
  • 📚NSLS Content & Curriculum ▶ 8:35 — Both advisors cited the content as the core reason they stay with NSLS. Jennifer noted that TAMUCC has built its entire leadership infrastructure around it and is 'fully invested.' Lauren emphasized lifetime membership and optional advanced programming as unique differentiators.
  • 🎖️Grad Gear Incentive Program ▶ 19:12 — TAMUCC created a locally-built incentive program called Grad Gear that rewards students for attending community service events, career development events, and campus traditions — including earning a stole paid for by the chapter. Lauren cited incentives as a major driver of student engagement.
  • 📊High Induction Rate ▶ 16:20 — The chapter consistently converts 45–50% of new members to induction, exceeding the prior 40% pillar benchmark. Jennifer attributed this to intentional personal outreach, including direct texts to students about missing steps.
❌ What Isn't Working
  • 🗂️Loss of SNT Manager Portal ▶ 10:00 — Both advisors expressed frustration that the SNT Manager portal was deprecated. Lauren rebuilt its functionality in a custom Google Site. The original tool automatically organized SNT weeks, facilitator rosters, contact info, and communication links — all now handled manually.
  • 📧Dual Email & Invitation Confusion ▶ 12:57 — Students registering with personal vs. school emails causes communication breakdowns — students miss orientation emails or don't know they've already signed up. Reminder emails sent to already-registered members cause confusion and generate extra inbound contacts to advisors.
  • 📱Fragmented Communication Channels ▶ 23:17 — Lauren described running communications across iEngage, GroupMe, NSLS platform emails, and direct texts simultaneously. Students experience communication overload and begin ignoring messages. Lauren explicitly said she anticipated Society would offer 'one designated spot for it all.'
⚠️ Key Signal▶ 9:55 — SNT Scheduling Automation is the Highest-Value Near-Term Win: Lauren and Jennifer gave the most detailed, unprompted feedback about SNT operations — describing a multi-step manual process involving facilitator availability collection, manual roster assignment, and separate communication setup that Lauren had to replicate herself in a custom Google Site after the portal was removed. Jennifer immediately asked whether Society would allow pre-loading facilitator time slots and setting enrollment caps, signaling this is a workflow they are actively trying to solve. Lauren confirmed she would be open to piloting Society starting with SNT scheduling alone, even if everything else remained in-person. This represents the clearest, most specific product need with the highest potential to reduce advisor labor and build trust for broader platform adoption.
