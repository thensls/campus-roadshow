About Lehigh University
  • 🏛️Lehigh University is a private four-year institution in Bethlehem, PA, with an institutional priority framed as being 'first, the best, or the only,' with particular emphasis on career outcomes and interdisciplinary programming. ▶ 6:14

Chapter Status
  • 🆕Lehigh's NSLS chapter began approximately two years ago, officially became a recognized student organization in Spring 2025, and completed its first full academic year of chapter programming in 2024–2025.
  • 💻The chapter is classified as an online chapter, though the e-board and students express a strong preference for in-person engagement where possible.
  • 🌱Community-building infrastructure is still developing — the Community Service Chair role is newer, and building community within the chapter is cited as a priority for upcoming semesters.

Meeting Highlights Full Meeting Report ›

✅ What's Working / What Landed
  • 🏆Student Empowerment Model ▶ 4:00 — Freemont highlighted that the NSLS structure empowers students to truly lead — co-presidents Michael and Sabine meet directly with the CSR and drive chapter activity independently. She called this a core value that should never be removed.
  • 🌟Dream Job Feature ▶ 38:22 — Freemont responded positively to the career clarity dream job generator, saying she loves that it makes students 'think big' and visualize future possibilities — aligning with her philosophy around aspirational career exploration.
  • 🔒Privacy Controls on Profile Data ▶ 36:45 — Freemont explicitly appreciated that student scores and self-ratings would not be visible to peers, stating she wants students to be honest in ranking themselves without worrying about judgment from others.
❌ What Isn't Working
  • 🗺️Missing Entry-Level Pathways ▶ 39:16 — Freemont flagged that while dream jobs are aspirational and useful, students often don't know how to get from where they are now to a senior-level role. She asked whether the platform would support reverse-engineering career paths or surfacing entry-level stepping-stone roles.
  • 📅SNT Coordination Overhead ▶ 45:07 — Freemont noted that the current SNT process involves heavy upfront coordination — preference sheets, schedule matching, group assignments — and suggested there is room to simplify that structure, implying the current process may be more rigid than necessary.
  • 🔄No Prompt to Revisit Assessments ▶ 37:54 — Freemont raised the question of whether students are encouraged to retake personality assessments over time as they grow. Lauren confirmed they can, but acknowledged students are not currently prompted to do so — flagged as a gap in the ongoing engagement design.
⚠️ Key Signal▶ 48:41 — Early-Stage Career Clarity as Institutional Differentiator: Freemont articulated a clear and compelling vision: if Society reaches students in their first or sophomore year, it could accelerate major exploration, campus involvement alignment, and internship planning — not just at graduation but throughout the undergraduate journey. She connected this directly to Lehigh's institutional goal of leading in outcomes, framing Society as a potential vehicle for providing a 'different entity' talking to students about career exploration earlier than traditional career services can reach them. This is a strong product-market fit signal tied to Lehigh's stated strategic priorities.
