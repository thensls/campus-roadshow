Meeting Highlights Full Meeting Report ›

✅ What's Working / What Landed
  • 🏅 ▶ 11:26 — Katelyn emphasized that recognition resonates universally regardless of student age — she observes NSLS badges being shared on LinkedIn during invitation cycles and hears directly from older students (including those in their 70s) that being recognized feels meaningful even when career outcomes are less relevant.
  • 🌐 ▶ 11:45 — Katelyn explicitly said the online component is foundational to CSU's ability to have a chapter at all, and urged NSLS to never reduce virtual access — only increase it.
  • 📧 ▶ 7:55 — Katelyn uses the chapter leader dashboard bulk email tool regularly — approximately twice a year — to reach new members and cross-promote the CSU Facebook group, and reported strong response rates (300+ replies in one campaign).
  • 🤖 ▶ 24:00 — Katelyn responded enthusiastically to the AI coach and career clarity demo, connecting it immediately to her career services background and asking detailed questions about data portability, campus agent integration, and networking applications — signaling strong conceptual alignment.
❌ What Isn't Working
  • 📬 ▶ 12:48 — When Katelyn sends bulk emails to large member groups (sometimes close to 1,000 students), all replies collapse into a single thread rather than individual conversations, making it very difficult to manage responses and follow up with specific students.
  • 🗺️ ▶ 21:06 — Students frequently contact Katelyn after joining believing they are already members, unaware they have not completed induction. She described wanting a visible 'you are here' step-by-step map — not just a next-step prompt, but a full breakdown shown in a simple, non-overwhelming format.
  • 🔗 ▶ 53:19 — Katelyn needed clarification on whether Society would be a separate login or registration path from existing NSLS membership — reflecting broader concern about adding friction or extra clicks for already time-constrained adult learners.
⚠️ Key Signal▶ 43:08 — Career Services Data Crossover Creates Multi-Department Value Proposition: Katelyn proactively flagged that Society's career readiness data — particularly student-inputted job titles, dream jobs, and career direction history — would be of direct interest to CSU's Career Services and Compliance teams, who are already tracking degree-to-employment outcomes. She drew explicit parallels to existing tools like Focuscareer and Big Interview, positioning Society as a complementary but differentiated layer that could serve students earlier in their journey. This cross-departmental data appetite is a strong signal that Society could be positioned not just as an NSLS engagement tool but as a campus-wide career readiness infrastructure play at CSU.
