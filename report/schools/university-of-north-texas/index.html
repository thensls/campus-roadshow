✅ What's Working / What Landed
  • 💻Online Accessibility ▶ 9:00 — Sydney cited seamless online and asynchronous access as the single most important thing she does not want to lose — noting NSLS has the highest student interaction of any program in her office because of it.
  • 🤝Induction & Invitation Success ▶ 0:59 — Jakita noted UNT has a tremendous amount of success with induction and invitation, describing it as a fantastic chapter.
  • 🧭AI Career Clarity Demo ▶ 1:01:37 — Sydney rated her excitement a 9/10 and said the platform is more valuable than what students currently get, noting she had independently conceived a nearly identical AI career exploration tool.
❌ What Isn't Working
  • 📱App Reliability Issues ▶ 10:41 — Sydney reported consistent issues over two years with the NSLS app — wrong event times, inaccessible content sections, and slow load times — leading the chapter to stop recommending the app to students entirely.
  • 📋Missing Skill-Level Outcome Data ▶ 48:44 — Sydney lacks data on specific leadership skills or learning outcomes students gain from NSLS participation, making it harder to justify budget requests, align reports to NACE competencies, and defend the program's value to administration.
  • 💸Cost Sensitivity for First-Gen Students ▶ 1:00:19 — Sydney flagged that the current $95 join fee already causes hesitation among their 40% first-generation student population, and expressed concern that any price increase or modular add-on cost could further reduce participation.
⚠️ Key Signal▶ 41:10 — Independent Parallel Invention — Sydney Already Built This in Her Head: Before seeing the Society demo, Sydney had independently conceived and pitched an AI-driven career exploration tool — nearly identical to the Clarity track — during a campus workforce readiness ideation session. She recognized Society in real time and said 'I was like, this is literally what I was thinking.' This is not a passive prospect; she is a natural internal champion who has already done the intellectual work of selling this concept to herself and has institutional motivation through the president's career readiness mandate and NACE adoption to push it forward.
