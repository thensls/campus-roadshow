About La Roche University

  • 📈Post-COVID re-engagement is the overarching institutional priority, with leadership pushing to ramp up campus events and community involvement.
  • 🤝A new VP of Financial Aid (an NSLS alum) and a new head of student development (who proactively pledged to include NSLS in all campus club activity) represent meaningful new internal allies.

Meeting Highlights Full Meeting Report ›

✅ What's Working / What Landed
  • 🌟Student Transformation Stories — Paige Grogan cited personal and observed transformation as her primary source of motivation — her own experience overcoming shyness and watching her student president grow from shy to confident validated NSLS's core value proposition for her.
  • 📊Career Readiness Score & Advisor Dashboard — Paige Grogan responded positively to the advisor dashboard and member data view, immediately connecting it to her need to understand members' majors, grade levels, and engagement levels when she doesn't already know them personally.
  • 🎯SNT Matching Automation — Paige Grogan reacted positively to the SNT group-matching feature, noting she has members who ask what benefits they get from NSLS beyond membership — this gave her a concrete answer.
  • 🤖AI Coach & Clarity Track — The Clarity Track and AI Coach resonated as a post-induction value-add Paige Grogan could offer students who question the benefit of continued engagement.
❌ What Isn't Working
  • ⏱️SNT Submission Timing Window — Students who complete SNTs but miss the weekly submission window cannot log completed meetings retroactively. A student who attended a meeting but couldn't submit due to schedule conflicts (sports practice, forgot laptop) was blocked from logging the activity the following week.
  • 📱App Accessibility & Habit Formation — Students frequently don't have the app on their phone or forget their laptop, leading to missed submissions even when the underlying behavior (meeting with their group) occurred.
  • 📉No Peer Benchmarking by School Type — Paige Grogan expressed a clear unmet need: she wants to see engagement and success rate data filtered by schools that match La Roche's profile (small, private, Catholic, suburban) to make the case to her VP, marketing, and admissions. She noted this is how La Roche benchmarks institutionally and she has no way to do this today.
⚠️ Key Signal — Peer Benchmarking by School Type as a Novel Product Signal: Paige Grogan surfaced a product need that Chris explicitly flagged as not previously heard: the ability to filter Society's reporting dashboard by comparable school profiles (small, private, Catholic, suburban). This is not just a personal preference — it directly mirrors how La Roche benchmarks institutionally for enrollment and retention decisions. Delivering this capability could unlock buy-in from her VP of Financial Aid, marketing, and admissions, all of whom already use peer-school comparisons as a standard decision-making lens.
