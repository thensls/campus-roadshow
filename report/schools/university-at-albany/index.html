Meeting Highlights Full Meeting Report ›

✅ What's Working / What Landed
  • 🤖AI Coach Concept ▶ 53:34 — Jermaine explicitly said he loves platforms that walk students through problems rather than just giving them answers, directly validating Society's guardrail-based coaching approach as differentiated from generic ChatGPT use.
  • 🎯Career Clarity Track ▶ 22:48 — The dream job generation and career direction framing resonated; Jermaine engaged substantively with how it differs from Focus 2 and interest-only tools, and acknowledged value for schools high in student population and low in resources.
  • 📋Advisor Dashboard & Career Readiness Score ▶ 27:45 — Sari's question about whether a real-life coach could see AI coach results and reinforce them was directly addressed by the advisor dashboard concept, which both advisors found directionally promising for enabling more efficient in-person advising conversations.
  • 🔗Flexible Login & Email Portability ▶ 55:38 — Jermaine validated the ability to tie personal and school emails together as solving a real platform continuity problem, noting that institutional emails typically expire three to six months after graduation and cause platforms to go haywire.
❌ What Isn't Working
  • ⚙️Back-End Usability Concerns ▶ 49:15 — Both advisors independently raised concern about what platform management looks like on the back end. Sari cited time pressure and shifting portfolios in higher ed; Jermaine warned that higher ed institutions become graveyards for platforms that staff do not use or that lose their champion when someone leaves.
  • 🔍StrengthsFinder Not Integrated ▶ 23:20 — Jermaine noted that the career education side at UAlbany relies heavily on Gallup StrengthsFinder as their primary tool, and the values exercise reminded him of Strength Quest. The absence of a native integration is a gap relative to existing workflows.
  • 📉NSLS Back-End Data Integrity ▶ 48:16 — Jermaine flagged that the existing NSLS platform back-end appeared to show implausibly high user numbers — he cited what looked like 72,000 users — and concluded that no one had been doing the maintenance needed, describing the data as uncleaned and inaccurate.
  • 🏫Unclear Career Center Integration Path ▶ 42:43 — Jermaine noted that career services structures vary widely across universities — some report to student affairs, some to academic affairs — and advised that NSLS will need to specifically engage the career education side of the house at each school rather than assuming a single integration model.
⚠️ Key Signal▶ 44:13 — Jermaine as Unexpected Career Tech Bridge: Jermaine Harris arrived new to NSLS but immediately engaged as a sophisticated career technology evaluator, raising nuanced questions about interests vs. capabilities, back-end usability, staff adoption risk, and employer data limitations. Most notably, he volunteered at the end of the call to share the meeting recording with his career education team so they could see the platform — an unsolicited internal amplification signal. His framing that Society should partner directly with career education, not just student engagement, provides a clear strategic directive for how NSLS should sequence its outreach at UAlbany and similar institutions.
