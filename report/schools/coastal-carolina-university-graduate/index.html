✅ What's Working / What Landed
  • 🎯Post-Induction 'Carrot' Model ▶ 14:38 — Daphne explicitly said she liked using Society as an added benefit after members complete programming, and Maryanna reinforced it would protect the strong in-person culture already built.
  • 🔍Career Clarity Track ▶ 36:22 — Maryanna said she wanted to have her niece test it and described the career clarity output as validating or course-correcting for users at any career stage.
  • 🤖AI Coach Personalization ▶ 2:59 — Chris walked through how the coach retains context across values, personality, and goals — advisors did not push back on this feature and engaged positively with the walkthrough.
❌ What Isn't Working
  • 🔐Magic Link / Auth Bug ▶ 26:11 — Daphne Holland and Megan Grandey both experienced verification link failures repeatedly. Chris captured screenshots and flagged it as the first documented bug. Workaround was signing in with Google/Gmail.
  • 🏫No In-Person Facilitation Support ▶ 10:37 — Chris acknowledged the legacy dashboard supports in-person facilitation better than Society currently does, and said he could not commit that in-person support features would be ready by fall.
  • 📚Articulation Agreement / Legacy Course Lock-In ▶ 13:37 — Maryanna flagged that Coastal Carolina has an articulation agreement covering advanced and executive courses, and Chris confirmed those courses will remain in the legacy system through fall — effectively locking that portion of programming out of Society.
⚠️ Key Signal▶ 20:18 — Graduate Chapter as Parallel Async Pilot Track: Maryanna Laferriere proactively proposed using the Coastal Carolina graduate chapter — led by Tyrone McKenney — as a parallel pilot track running Society as its primary engagement platform in a fully asynchronous model, while the undergraduate chapter pilots it as a post-induction benefit. Chris affirmed this was a valuable approach that would generate comparative feedback from two meaningfully different deployment models. Tyrone McKenney's chapter represents the highest-potential async-first use case in the current pilot cohort, though he was not present to confirm directly.
