✅ What's Working / What Landed
  • 🌐National Online Events ▶ 23:31 — Dr. Quintero specifically cited the addition of national online events as highly beneficial for NLC's large part-time and dual-credit student population, noting that scheduling flexibility helps students stay engaged even when in-person attendance is difficult.
  • 💰Scholarship and Financial Incentives ▶ 17:45 — Dr. Quintero identified scholarship opportunities as a key recruiting technique for NLC, describing it as a meaningful benefit that students respond to and that advisors actively use to drive induction.
  • 🎯GPA Invitation Threshold ▶ 20:27 — Dr. Quintero expressed appreciation for the GPA-based invitation model (NLC uses 2.75), viewing it as an incentive for academic improvement and a mechanism that ties engagement to classroom success rather than open enrollment.
❌ What Isn't Working
  • ⏰Advisor Bandwidth Constraints ▶ 49:14 — Dr. Quintero acknowledged that NLC struggles with having enough time to manage all NSLS requirements, signaling that the advising workload is a real friction point, particularly given that she advises from the faculty side without dedicated student life staff present.
  • 📋In-Person Event and Platform Integration Clarity ▶ 53:53 — Dr. Quintero asked multiple clarifying questions about how Society would interact with existing in-person events like orientation and Leadership Training Day, indicating that the relationship between the digital platform and physical induction steps was not immediately clear.
⚠️ Key Signal▶ 18:33 — Faculty-Led Retention Focus Creates Strong Product Alignment: Dr. Quintero's engagement with Society is grounded in observed persistence data rather than programmatic tradition — she joined and stayed with NSLS specifically because she sees engagement outside the classroom as a measurable driver of student success and retention. Her detailed implementation questions about how Society interacts with in-person induction events signal close operational attention rather than passive interest. She confirmed individual pilot interest and proactively clarified that each Alamo chapter would decide independently, positioning NLC to move without waiting for district-wide alignment.
