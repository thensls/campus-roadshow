Meeting Highlights Full Meeting Report ›

✅ What's Working / What Landed
  • 🎯Career Clarity Track Resonance ▶ 33:48 — Rebecca immediately connected the Clarity track to her 'career exploration' appointments, noting that students could arrive having already worked through the process — reducing the burden on advisors and improving appointment quality.
  • 📊Advisor Dashboard & Progress Tracking ▶ 24:44 — The concept of a dedicated advisor dashboard with cross-cohort progress tracking resonated with Rebecca, who had recently spent significant manual effort auditing her student roster against the SIS.
  • 🤝SNT Matching & Completion Tool ▶ 31:25 — Rebecca confirmed SNT completion is a known gap for YSU, calling the automated matching and scheduling feature 'beneficial' and affirming it addresses a real problem on her chapter's list.
  • 🏫School Agent Concept ▶ 28:51 — The idea of an AI agent that could surface career services appointments directly to students — reducing the 'why do I need to go talk to them' barrier — visibly resonated with Rebecca given her commuter campus context.
❌ What Isn't Working
  • 📋Manual Roster Management ▶ 3:57 — Rebecca spent significant time manually cross-referencing the NSLS student list against Banner Hero ICO SIS to identify only actively enrolled students — a tedious process she described as 'a huge piece' that the platform should eventually automate.
  • 🚗Commuter Campus Engagement Gap ▶ 6:05 — The vast majority of YSU students commute, leave campus immediately after class, and do not attend evening or late-night events, making in-person programming and traditional engagement strategies largely ineffective.
  • 📏NSLS Scale vs. Institutional Reporting Needs ▶ 33:00 — Rebecca noted that fewer than 300 students are NSLS members out of 12,000 enrolled, making it difficult to present NSLS-derived career readiness data as meaningful at the institutional level without broader platform access.
⚠️ Key Signal▶ 32:07 — Career Exploration Appointments as Primary Use Case: Rebecca explicitly identified the 'career exploration appointment' — where students arrive with little direction — as a recurring pain point that Society's Clarity track could directly address by preparing students before they walk in the door. She also noted Ohio's state-level mandate around workforce readiness and work-based learning as institutional pressure points that completion and career readiness data could help her office respond to. Expanding platform access beyond NSLS members to all 12,000 enrolled students was flagged as the condition under which institutional-level reporting would become truly valuable.
